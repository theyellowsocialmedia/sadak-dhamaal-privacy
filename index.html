Sadak Dhamaal: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Sadak Dhamaal is a mobile game published by The Yellow Social Media ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

Summary

We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information. The game has no login, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Your best scores, total distance, selected vehicle, character and game settings are saved only in the app's local storage on your device.
  • If you add songs from your phone to play during a ride, the app keeps a copy of those audio files in its own local storage on your device so your playlists work next time. These files never leave your phone.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data from Android settings, deletes all of this.

Permissions

  • Vibration: a short vibration when you crash.
  • File picker: only when you tap "Phone se gaane chuno", so you can choose songs yourself. The app cannot see any other files.
  • Internet: required by the Android web engine the game runs on. The game itself does not send any data anywhere.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
